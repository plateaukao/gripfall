TOUCHDOWN

Climb. Hook back. Land.

The ramp bends back toward your starting side. Hold ↑ for suction through the curve, then hold ↓ to fire the lift jets. During the demo, highlighted buttons show the commands being used.

Space (hold)
Drive forward along the ground and ramp
↑ (hold)
Suction pulls the car against the ramp; release to let it wind down. This does not fire jets.
↓ (hold)
Fire jets downward to push the car upward and slow its fall
← / →
Left / right landing thrust
W (optional)
Downward thrust to descend faster
A / D
Front / rear lift jets to adjust pitch
Level assist
Automatically uses pitch jets; directional thrust stays manual
R
Retry

Land inside the lit zone with a level body, vertical speed below 4.2 m/s, and horizontal speed below 5.2 m/s. Jets use fuel. Vacuum works only at the surface.
